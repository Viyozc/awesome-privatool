## Why privacy-first?

Most "free online tools" upload your files to a server to process them. That means your photos, PDFs, and documents — often containing personal or sensitive data — leave your device and sit on someone else's infrastructure, where they may be logged, cached, or retained.

The tools in this list use **WebAssembly and browser APIs** to do all processing locally. Nothing is uploaded. This is verifiable, not a promise: open DevTools → Network, run any tool, and watch for zero upload requests.
